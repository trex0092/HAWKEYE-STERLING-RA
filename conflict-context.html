Record the review in the restricted compliance case

No case data is collected or stored on this page. Do not put customer names, identity documents, supplier details, case references or credentials into public GitHub files or external URLs.

  1. Explain the relevance to the declared mineral origin, supplier, intermediary or transport route. Geography alone is not a finding against a person or organisation.
  2. Record the originating publisher and URL, underlying evidence date, analytical review date, access time and region. Keep dates separate; page modification or retrieval time is not the evidence date.
  3. Write an attributed assessment, preserve uncertainty, and record supporting documentation through the approved case process. Allegations are not verified criminal findings.
  4. Check source lineage. An ArmedConflicts.org summary derived from CFR is one originating assessment, not two independent corroborations. Do not duplicate articles across aggregators.
  5. Record the reviewer, next action and any required human approval. Do not close MLRO tasks, release a hold, or change a risk score from these links.
