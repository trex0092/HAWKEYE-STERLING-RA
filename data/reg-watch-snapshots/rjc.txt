and watch practices scroll to explore rooted in responsibility, driven by purpose who we are we are the world&#8217;s leading standard-setting organisation for the jewellery and watch industry founded in 2005, by 14 organisations including de beers, tiffany &amp; co., and cartier, we unite over 2,100 companies across the entire global watch and jewellery supply chain, to promote responsible practices and ethical standards. we advance trust, transparency, and sustainability, benefiting consumers, communities, and the environment. our story what we do the jewellery and watch industry has a larger ambition to contribute to a better, fairer world we drive change by helping companies embed responsible practices into daily operations. members follow one or more of three standards: the code of practices (cop), chain of custody (coc), and laboratory grown material standard (lgms). by implementing our standards, members support the un 2030 agenda and the 17 sustainable development goals. our standards become a member membership is your pathway to sustainable growth​ sustainability is a journey of continuous improvement. rjc membership gives you the opportunity to accelerate yours. what differentiates us is that we have one solution for businesses, big or small, to integrate sustainability in its operations. our standards cover human rights, labour rights, health and safety,
