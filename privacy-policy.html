1. What this tool is

Hawkeye Sterling is an AML/CFT entity risk-assessment tool used internally by this firm's compliance function under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 and Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025. This policy covers this website only — it does not replace the firm's own data-processing policy governing customer due-diligence obligations (see the firm's internal Data Privacy Policy).

2. Data storage and processing

This page states what the application actually does, verified directly against its source code:

  • Case data you enter (entity names, risk factors, assessment verdicts) is stored in your browser's local storage by default and is not sent anywhere automatically.
  • Backup and case tracking: when an assessment is marked complete, or when the register/activity log is mirrored, a summary is sent to this firm's private Asana workspace so a copy survives off-device and case tracking works across sessions. This is firm infrastructure, not a third party acting independently — treat it the same as any other internal case-management system.
  • AI Advisor: if you use the Advisor feature, your question and any case context you type is sent to Anthropic's API (the provider behind Claude) to generate a response. Do not enter more identifying detail than the question requires.
  • Analytics (optional): this site can use Google Analytics 4 to understand usage. It only runs if you click Accept on the banner shown on first visit; declining or ignoring it means no analytics script loads. If accepted, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies to distinguish visits. See Google's own privacy policy for how it processes that data.

3. What this site does not do

This site does not sell data, does not run advertising, and (outside the optional analytics above) does not load any third-party tracking script. Its Content-Security-Policy blocks any script or network connection this document does not name.

4. Your choices

You can decline or later revoke analytics consent by clearing this site's local storage in your browser settings. Case data can be exported or deleted from within the app itself.
