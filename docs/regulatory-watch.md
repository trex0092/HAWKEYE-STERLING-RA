# Regulatory Watch

Automated monitoring of the regulations, circulars, procedures and guidance the
Hawkeye Sterling app relies on — **worldwide coverage, UAE-weighted**. When a
monitored source changes, the system opens a **pull request** so the change can
be reviewed and applied to the app's content (the Regulatory Q&A answers and
Super Tools citations in `assets/super-data.js`, and the country/risk data in
`index.html`).

> **Why a pull request, not an auto-edit?** Regulator-grade wording must be
> verified against the primary source before it goes live. Detection is fully
> automatic; the wording change is a reviewed decision. (Country black/grey
> **list** moves are handled separately by the [FATF Watchdog](../scripts/fatf-watchdog.mjs).)

## How it works

1. **Schedule** — `.github/workflows/regulatory-watch.yml` runs every **Monday 06:00 UTC**, and on demand via *Run workflow*.
2. **Fingerprint** — `scripts/reg-watch.mjs` fetches each source in [`data/reg-sources.json`](../data/reg-sources.json), strips markup, normalises the text, and hashes it. The hash is compared with the last-seen value in `data/reg-watch-state.json`. Markup-only churn does not move the hash; a real text change does. Fetch errors (404, timeouts) are recorded but never counted as a content change, so they cannot raise a false PR.
3. **On change** — the workflow opens/updates a PR on branch `regulatory-watch/update` carrying the updated fingerprint state and a change report (which sources moved, with links).
4. **Optional AI draft** — if an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` repository secret is set, `scripts/reg-draft.mjs` fetches each changed page and asks Claude (`claude-sonnet-4-6`) to draft a reviewer-facing proposal — what changed and which app entries likely need updating — written to `docs/research/auto/REG-UPDATE-<date>.md` and included in the PR. Without the secret, the PR is detection-only. The AI step **never** edits `super-data.js` directly.

### First-time setup

Run the workflow once with **`mode = seed`** (*Run workflow → mode: seed*) to
record the current fingerprints without raising change noise. Thereafter the
weekly run flags only genuine changes. Optionally add the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`
secret to enable AI-drafted proposals.

### Adding or removing a source

Edit [`data/reg-sources.json`](../data/reg-sources.json). Each entry needs a
stable `id`, a `name`, a `url`, a `type`, and a `narrative` (what it is, why it
is watched, and what it feeds in the app). The unit tests
(`test/reg-watch.test.mjs`, run in CI) enforce unique ids, valid URLs, and that
every source carries a narrative.

| **UAE Ministry of Economy (MoE) — DNFBP / DPMS supervisor** | Federal supervisor for Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions under Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 (as amended by No. 26 of 2021) and Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 — covering Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones (DPMS), real-estate brokers, auditors and corporate service providers. A change typically signals new filing formats, deadlines, narrative-quality expectations or typologies. A change usually means a new NRA cycle, revised national risk ratings, or strategy documents that supervisors will operationalize. ### Global

| Source | Narrative |
| --- | --- |
| **FATF — guidance, recommendations & news** | The intergovernmental AML/CFT/CPF standard-setter whose 40 Recommendations and Methodology anchor every national regime, including the UAE's. This entry watches the publications feed: Recommendation and Interpretive-Note revisions, mutual-evaluation reports, risk-based-approach guidance (including DPMS-relevant guidance), typologies and best-practice papers. NOT the black/grey country lists — the separate FATF Watchdog diffs those semantically. A change usually means revised standards or new guidance the app's citations must track. Feeds: most Q&A citations, tool reference lists, and methodology content. Fetch note: bot-protected; monitored via the web-capture fallback. |
| **Wolfsberg Group** | Association of thirteen global banks setting de-facto industry standards for financial-crime risk management. Publishes the Correspondent Banking Due Diligence Questionnaire (CBDDQ), the Wolfsberg Principles, payment-transparency and sanctions-screening guidance, and periodic FAQs. A change usually means an updated CBDDQ version or new due-diligence expectations that counterparties will start requesting. Feeds: correspondent-banking and CDD answers, and questionnaire-handling tools. |
| **Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI)** | Industry programme (under the Responsible Business Alliance) operating the Responsible Minerals Assurance Process (RMAP) for smelters/refiners and maintaining the Conflict Minerals Reporting Template (CMRT) and Extended Minerals Reporting Template (EMRT). Publishes audit-protocol revisions, template releases, and conformant/active smelter lists. A change usually means new template versions or audit-protocol updates that supply-chain documentation must adopt. Feeds: RMI/minerals answers and template-version references in the sourcing tools. |
| **LBMA — Responsible Sourcing** | The London Bullion Market Association's responsible-sourcing programme for the Good Delivery List. Publishes Responsible Gold/Silver Guidance versions, assurance and country-of-origin requirements, Good Delivery rule changes, and incident notices affecting refiners. A change usually means a new RGG version or refiner-status decisions that gold-sourcing due-diligence steps must reflect. Feeds: LBMA answers, gold-sourcing logic, and refiner-verification steps. |
| **OECD — Responsible Mineral Supply Chains (CAHRA)** | Owner of the OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas — the five-step framework (incl. Annex II risks) that LBMA, DMCC, RJC and RMI programmes all implement — plus the Gold Supplement and MNE Guidelines. Monitored via the Responsible Business Conduct topic hub on oecd.org (the legacy mneguidelines.oecd.org host permanently bot-blocks non-browser clients). A change usually means revised guidance, new implementation reports, or CAHRA-methodology updates. Feeds: OECD answers, the high-risk-country / ASM risk floor, and Annex II risk mapping. |
| **US OFAC — Recent Actions** | The US Treasury's sanctions authority; its Recent Actions feed announces SDN/non-SDN designations and delistings, new or amended programs, General Licenses, FAQs and 50-percent-rule interpretations. UAE gold-trade relevance is direct: secondary-sanctions exposure and USD-clearing risk. A change usually means designations or guidance with same-day compliance impact. Feeds: sanctions answers and the escalation/CFA logic. Note: the machine-readable SDN/consolidated lists are separately fetched and screened daily by the sanctions pipelines; this entry watches announcements and guidance. |
| **UN Security Council — Consolidated List** | The UN's consolidated sanctions list page spanning the 1267/1989/2253 (ISIL/Al-Qaida), 1988 (Taliban), 1718 (DPRK) and other committee regimes — implemented in the UAE without delay under Cabinet Resolution No. 74 of 2020. A change usually means listings/delistings that trigger immediate freeze obligations locally. Feeds: sanctions/TF/PF answers and freeze logic. Note: the XML list itself is screened daily by the sanctions pipelines; this entry watches the page for regime-level announcements. |
| **EU — Financial Sanctions** | The European Commission's financial-sanctions hub: the consolidated list regime, ownership-and-control ('50%+') guidance, best-practice papers and the Blocking Statute (Reg. 2271/96). A change usually means new restrictive-measure packages or interpretive guidance affecting EU-nexus counterparties. Feeds: EU ownership-and-control answers and blocking-statute content. Note: the consolidated CSV list is separately screened daily. |
| **UK OFSI — Consolidated List of Targets** | HM Treasury's Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation: the UK consolidated list under the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018, licensing decisions, enforcement (monetary penalties) and guidance. A change usually means UK designations or guidance relevant to correspondent and trade-finance exposure. Feeds: UK-designation answers. Note: the ConList CSV is separately screened daily. |
| **Egmont Group of FIUs** | The 170+-member global network of Financial Intelligence Units governing FIU-to-FIU information exchange through its Principles for Information Exchange and secure channels; publishes operational guidance, typology projects and membership decisions (suspensions matter for cooperation routes). A change usually means new exchange standards or membership events affecting cross-border requests. Feeds: Egmont information-request answers and international-cooperation tools. |
| **Basel AML Index** | The Basel Institute on Governance's composite country ML/TF risk score (built from FATF evaluations, TI, WEF and other inputs), re-published annually with methodology revisions. A change usually means the annual release or an interim revision. Feeds: the country-risk weighting in the EWRA and customer risk-rating recalibration — score changes should trigger a jurisdiction-risk review in data/jurisdiction-risk.json. |
| **UNODC — Money Laundering & Organised Crime** | The UN Office on Drugs and Crime's AML portal: custodian of the Vienna and Palermo Conventions, publisher of predicate-offence and organised-crime research, the Global Programme against Money Laundering (GPML) outputs and model legislation. A change usually means new typology research or convention-related guidance. Feeds: typology, predicate-offence and international-cooperation answers. |
| **EU — High-Risk Third Countries (AML/CFT)** | The European Commission's list of third countries with strategic AML/CFT deficiencies (Delegated Regulation under the 4th/5th AMLD, aligned with but not identical to FATF's grey list), which triggers mandatory enhanced due diligence for EU-obliged entities on any listed-country nexus. A change usually means additions/removals with direct EDD consequences. Feeds: high-risk-jurisdiction EDD answers and the EWRA country-risk weighting — additions/removals must be mirrored the same week. |
| **Responsible Jewellery Council (RJC)** | The jewellery and watch industry's standards and certification body: the Code of Practices (COP — full-scope responsible business including AML/KYC provisions), the Chain of Custody (CoC) standard, and the Laboratory Grown Material Standard (LGMS), each with assessment manuals and accredited-auditor requirements. Publishes standards revisions, audit/certification-requirement changes (e.g. Standards & Audit Changes information packs), governance announcements and progress reports. A change usually means revised certification requirements members must meet at next audit. Feeds: gold/jewellery sector answers, responsible-sourcing tools, and RJC-certification citations. |
