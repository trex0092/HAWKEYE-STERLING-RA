# Transaction Monitoring & Reporting Policy (STR · DPMSR · wire transfers)

**Owner:** MLRO (accountable) · Compliance Officer (operational)
**Approver:** Board of Directors
**Status:** DRAFT — for Board approval (open-actions item 18)
**Version:** 0.1 · **Review cadence:** annually, and on any change to thresholds, goAML report specifications or the monitoring estate.

**Regulatory basis:** Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 (reporting of suspicion;
Article 25 tipping-off) · Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025 (wire-transfer
data, record retention) · MoE AML/CFT Guidelines for DNFBPs (September 2025).

---

## 1. Two duties that are often confused

| | **DPMSR** | **STR** |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Threshold: cash **or** international wire ≥ **AED 55,000**, single or cumulative linked | Reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF/PF |
| Threshold | Yes — AED 55,000 | **None** — any amount |
| Suspicion required | No — filed regardless | Yes |
| Channel | goAML | goAML |

**Both may apply to the same transaction.** Where a transaction is at or above
the threshold *and* raises suspicion, **file both**. A DPMSR is never a
substitute for an STR.

## 2. Monitoring

Transactions are scrutinised for consistency with the customer's known profile,
the stated purpose of the relationship, and the source of funds. Monitoring
covers: value and frequency against profile; structuring below the AED 55,000
threshold; linked transactions across days or entities; third-party payments;
unusual routing; price or invoice discrepancies against market value
(trade-based money laundering); and any pattern with no apparent commercial
logic.

**Linked-transaction rule.** Cumulative linked dealings by the same customer, or
by connected customers, are aggregated for threshold purposes. A series
deliberately kept below the threshold is itself a red flag and is assessed for
suspicion, not merely excluded from reporting.

**Current state — stated, not hidden.** The firm's automated
transaction-monitoring engine is built and **inactive**: no transaction feed is
wired to it. Until it is, monitoring runs under the documented manual
compensating control at
[`../aims/transaction-feed-compensating-control.md`](../aims/transaction-feed-compensating-control.md),
and the gap is tracked as open-actions item 6. Nothing in this policy should be
read as claiming automated coverage the firm does not yet have.

## 3. Wire transfers

For transfers at or above **AED 3,500**, full originator and beneficiary
information is obtained, recorded and transmitted with the transfer. An incoming
transfer lacking required information is not processed on the assumption it will
follow; it is escalated, and the omission is assessed for suspicion.

## 4. Escalation and the filing decision

1. Any employee who identifies an unusual or suspicious matter escalates it to
   the MLRO **immediately** and does not discuss it with the customer.
2. The MLRO is the **sole decision-maker** on filing. Commercial considerations
   are not an input to that decision.
3. The MLRO records the decision either way. A **no-action decision is
   documented** with its reasoning to the same standard as a filing — an
   examiner reads the no-action file, not just the filed reports.
4. Filing is made **without delay** once suspicion is formed. Waiting to gather
   a fuller picture is not a reason to delay a report.

## 5. Tipping-off

No employee discloses to the customer, or to any third party, that a report has
been or will be made, or that an investigation is underway (Article 25, Federal
Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025). This survives the end of the relationship, overrides
a data-subject access request, and applies to internal conversations outside the
need-to-know group. The advisory tooling used by the compliance function
enforces the same rule technically and withholds output that would breach it.

## 6. Filing procedure and logs

Step-by-step goAML filing, required fields, deadlines, confirmation numbers and
the internal log are in
[`str-dpmsr-filing-procedure.md`](str-dpmsr-filing-procedure.md). The filing log
records every STR, DPMSR, PNMR, CNMR and FFR, plus every documented no-action
decision, and is reviewed for completeness and timeliness at least annually.

## 7. Records

Reports, supporting analysis, the evidence pack and the confirmation number are
retained for at least five years and produced to a competent authority within
48 hours of request.
