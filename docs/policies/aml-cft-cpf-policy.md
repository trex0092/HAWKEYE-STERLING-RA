## 1. Purpose and standing

This is the firm's master AML/CFT/CPF instrument. Every other policy and
procedure in [`docs/policies/`](README.md) implements a part of it. Where any
subordinate document conflicts with this policy, this policy prevails, and the
conflict is a defect to be corrected at the next review.

The firm operates as a **Dealer in Precious Metals and Stones (DPMS)**, a
Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession under the UAE framework, and is
supervised by the Ministry of Economy. That status, not the firm's size, sets
the obligations below.

## 2. Three pillars, not two

Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 makes **proliferation financing a standalone
offence** (Article 3(3)) alongside money laundering and terrorist financing.
This policy therefore covers three pillars — AML, CFT **and CPF** — and every
control below is read as applying to all three unless it says otherwise. PF is
assessed separately in the risk assessment and covered separately in training;
see [`proliferation-financing-policy.md`](proliferation-financing-policy.md).

## 3. Policy statements

1. **Risk-based approach.** The firm assesses its ML/TF/PF risk across
   customers, products, delivery channels, geographies and counterparties, and
   allocates control effort to that risk. Method:
   [`risk-assessment-methodology.md`](risk-assessment-methodology.md).
2. **No relationship without due diligence.** No business relationship is
   established, and no qualifying occasional transaction is executed, before
   customer due diligence is complete. See
   [`customer-acceptance-cdd-policy.md`](customer-acceptance-cdd-policy.md).
3. **Sanctions are absolute.** The firm does not deal with a designated person
   or entity. A confirmed match freezes without delay and is reported. See
   [`sanctions-tfs-policy.md`](sanctions-tfs-policy.md).
4. **Suspicion is reported.** Reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF/PF are
   reported to the FIU through goAML, with no monetary threshold and without
   tipping off. See
   [`transaction-monitoring-reporting-policy.md`](transaction-monitoring-reporting-policy.md).
5. **Thresholds are reported.** Qualifying cash and international wire
   transactions at or above **AED 55,000** are filed as DPMSR regardless of
   suspicion. Both a DPMSR and an STR are filed where both apply.
6. **Records are kept and produced.** Minimum **five years**, produced to a
   competent authority within **48 hours** of request. See
   [`record-keeping-retention-policy.md`](record-keeping-retention-policy.md).
7. **Sourcing is responsible.** Gold and precious metals are sourced under the
   OECD five-step framework and LBMA Responsible Gold Guidance. See
   [`responsible-sourcing-policy.md`](responsible-sourcing-policy.md).
8. **People are trained.** Role-appropriate AML/CFT/CPF training is delivered
   and recorded. See
   [`training-awareness-policy.md`](training-awareness-policy.md).
9. **Concerns can be raised safely.** See
   [`whistleblowing-policy.md`](whistleblowing-policy.md).
10. **The programme is tested.** Independent review at least annually; findings
    are tracked to closure in the CAPA log.

## 4. Governance and accountability

| Body / role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Board** | Approves this policy and the risk appetite; receives the MLRO annual report; ratifies material changes |
| **Senior management** | Personally approves internal policies and oversees high-risk relationships (Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025) |
| **MLRO** | Owns the programme; sole decision-maker on filing, freezing and no-action; direct Board access |

| **Internal Audit** | Independent testing of design and operating effectiveness |

Full architecture, independence rules and the annual compliance calendar:
[`compliance-governance-charter.md`](compliance-governance-charter.md).

**Personal liability.** Under Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025, directors and
managers can face personal criminal liability for offences committed by the
entity's representatives, knowledge may be inferred from factual and objective
circumstances (Article 2(3)), and AML/CFT/CPF offences carry **no limitation
period** (Article 37). Compliance is not a delegable convenience.

## 5. Prohibited business

The firm will not:

- deal with a person or entity designated on any applicable sanctions list;
- open or maintain an anonymous or fictitious-name relationship;
- accept a customer whose beneficial ownership cannot be established;
- accept a transaction with no identifiable originator or beneficiary;
- proceed where the source of funds or wealth cannot be reasonably explained;
- deal in gold from a conflict-affected or high-risk area (CAHRA) without the
  OECD documentation the responsible-sourcing policy requires;
- tip off a customer or third party about a report made or to be made
  (Article 25, Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025).

These are the firm's zero-tolerance positions and mirror the appetite recorded
in [`../governance/risk-appetite-statement-2026.md`](../governance/risk-appetite-statement-2026.md).

## 6. Consequences of breach

Breach of this policy is a disciplinary matter and may be a criminal offence.
Administrative penalties under the framework run to **AED 5,000,000** per
violation, with licence suspension or revocation available to the supervisor,
and principal ML/TF/PF offences carry substantially higher exposure.
