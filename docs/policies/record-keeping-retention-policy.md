# Record-Keeping & Retention Policy

**Owner:** MLRO (accountable) · Compliance Officer (operational)
**Approver:** Board of Directors
**Status:** DRAFT — for Board approval (open-actions item 18)
**Version:** 0.1 · **Review cadence:** annually, and on any change to the retention obligation or the storage estate.

**Regulatory basis:** Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 (record-keeping;
Article 10 retention) · Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025 (minimum five years;
production to competent authorities within 48 hours) · Federal Decree-Law
No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL).

---

## 1. Two obligations at once

Records must be **kept long enough** to satisfy AML/CFT/CPF law and **no longer
than necessary** to satisfy data-protection law. Where the two conflict, the
AML/CFT/CPF retention obligation prevails for the records it covers, and the
lawful basis for continued processing is the firm's legal obligation. Nothing in
data-protection law requires or permits deletion of a record the firm is
obliged to retain.

## 2. What is retained, and for how long

| Record class | Retention | Clock starts |
|---|---|---|
| CDD / KYC files (identification, verification, UBO) | 5 years minimum | End of the business relationship |
| Occasional-transaction records | 5 years minimum | Date of the transaction |
| Transaction records and supporting documents | 5 years minimum | Date of the transaction |
| Screening evidence (hits, clears, the reasoning) | 5 years minimum | Date of screening |
| STR / DPMSR / PNMR / CNMR / FFR and their evidence packs | 5 years minimum | Date of filing |
| **No-action decisions** and their reasoning | 5 years minimum | Date of decision |
| Risk assessments (customer and business-wide) | 5 years minimum | Date superseded |
| Training records | 5 years minimum | Date of delivery |
| Independent audit reports and CAPA | 5 years minimum | Date of report |
| Supplier / KYS and provenance files | 5 years minimum | End of the supplier relationship |

Where a longer period applies — an ongoing investigation, a production order, or
a supervisory direction — the longer period governs and the record is placed
under a **retention hold** that suspends any scheduled purge.

## 3. Production on demand

Records must be **retrievable and produced to a competent authority within 48
hours** of request. Retention without retrievability is not compliance: storage
locations, indexing and the named person responsible for retrieval are recorded,
and the retrieval path is tested as part of the independent audit.

## 4. Integrity and security

Records are protected against loss, unauthorised alteration and unauthorised
access. Backup, restoration and export-integrity controls are in
[`../governance/backup-recovery.md`](../governance/backup-recovery.md); the
technical stores, lineage and purge mechanics are in
[`../governance/data-retention.md`](../governance/data-retention.md), which this
policy governs.

## 5. Deletion

Deletion happens only when the retention period has expired, no hold applies,
and the deletion is recorded. Ad-hoc deletion at a customer's request is refused
where an AML retention obligation applies; the refusal and its basis are
recorded, and the request is assessed for suspicion where the circumstances
warrant it. A data-subject request never overrides the tipping-off prohibition.
