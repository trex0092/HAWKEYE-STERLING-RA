# Independent Audit Policy (AML/CFT/CPF programme)

**Owner:** Internal Audit (operational) · Board of Directors (accountable)
**Approver:** Board of Directors
**Status:** DRAFT — for Board approval (open-actions item 18)
**Version:** 0.1 · **Review cadence:** annually.

**Regulatory basis:** Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025, Article 17 (independent
audit function) · Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025.

---

## 1. Independence

The review is performed by a person or firm independent of the compliance
function and of the business lines being tested. The MLRO does not audit the
MLRO's own programme. Where the firm's size makes internal independence
impractical, the review is outsourced — and the provider is engaged under
[`outsourcing-third-party-policy.md`](outsourcing-third-party-policy.md).

Findings go to the **Board**, not through the function being audited.

## 2. Frequency

At least **annually**, and additionally after: a material control failure; a
significant regulatory change; an enforcement action or supervisory finding; or
a material change to the business model.

## 3. Scope

1. **Design effectiveness** — do the policies and procedures actually address
   every obligation in the obligation register?
2. **Operating effectiveness** — are they followed in practice?
3. **Testing** — sample-based verification, not interview-based assurance.
4. **Gap identification** — where controls fail, or do not exist.
5. **Remediation tracking** — are prior findings closed, and did the fix work?

## 4. Method

**Planning.** Define scope and objectives; identify focus areas from the risk
assessment; obtain the regulatory checklist and the obligation register.

**Fieldwork.** At minimum:

| Area | Test |
|---|---|
| CDD files | Sample the greater of **10% or 30 files**; test completeness, UBO identification, risk rating, EDD where triggered |
| Screening | Test that list updates were processed within 24 hours; re-run a sample; check cleared hits carry discriminating reasoning |
| Monitoring alerts | Review a sample of closed alerts for decision quality |
| STR / DPMSR log | Completeness and timeliness; **read the no-action decisions** |
| TFS events | Freeze timing, reporting deadlines, release basis |
| Training | Records exist, cover the right audiences, and assessments were passed |
| Records | Retrieval within 48 hours, tested — not assumed |
| Policy pack | Version control, ownership, approval records, no repealed citations |
| Interviews | MLRO, Compliance Officer, front office — to corroborate the file evidence, not to replace it |

**Reporting.** Findings rated Critical / High / Medium / Low, each with root
cause, recommendation, owner and deadline; comparison against prior findings;
an overall maturity view.

**Follow-up.** Findings land in [`../aims/corrective-actions.md`](../aims/corrective-actions.md);
Critical and High findings are re-tested, not merely marked closed.

## 5. Reporting line and escalation

The audit report goes to the Board. A Critical finding is escalated immediately
on discovery rather than held for the report. Management responses are recorded
alongside the findings — including a decision to accept a risk, with reasons.

## 6. Current state — stated, not hidden

The programme, scope and CAPA route exist; the **first thematic review has not
run** (obligation OB-12, open-actions item 8). The audit programme document is
[`../aims/internal-audit.md`](../aims/internal-audit.md), and this policy is the
governing instrument above it.
