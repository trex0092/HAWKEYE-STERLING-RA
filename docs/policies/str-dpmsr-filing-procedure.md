# STR / DPMSR Filing Procedure (goAML)

**Owner:** MLRO (accountable) · Compliance Officer (operational)
**Approver:** MLRO
**Status:** DRAFT — for MLRO approval (open-actions item 18)
**Version:** 0.1 · **Review cadence:** annually, and on any change to goAML report specifications.

**Regulatory basis:** Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 · Cabinet Resolution
No. 134 of 2025 · UAE FIU goAML guidance. Policy parent:
[`transaction-monitoring-reporting-policy.md`](transaction-monitoring-reporting-policy.md).

## 1. Who does what

| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Identify and escalate the matter | Any employee |
| Assemble the evidence pack | Compliance Officer |
| Decide to file or not to file | **MLRO — sole decision-maker** |
| Submit on goAML | MLRO (or CO under written delegation) |
| Record confirmation and file the pack | Compliance Officer |

## 2. STR — suspicion-based

1. **Receive** the internal escalation. Do not contact the customer.
2. **Assemble**: customer file, ownership structure, transaction records,
   screening results, adverse-media findings, the analyst's narrative of what is
   unusual and why.
3. **Assess** against the profile: what was expected, what happened, what
   explanation exists, and why it does not resolve the concern.
4. **Decide.** If there are reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF/PF — file. There
   is no monetary threshold and no requirement to prove the predicate offence.
5. **Draft the narrative**: who, what, when, where, how much, through which
   accounts or channels, and *why it is suspicious*. Facts before conclusions;
   no speculation presented as fact; every assertion traceable to a document in
   the pack.
6. **Submit** through goAML, selecting the correct report type.
7. **Record** the confirmation number in the filing log; retain the pack for
   five years.
8. **Continue** to apply the tipping-off prohibition indefinitely.

**No-action decisions are filed too.** Where the MLRO decides not to report, the
reasoning is recorded in the same log with the same evidence pack. An
undocumented no-action decision is indistinguishable, on inspection, from a
control that never ran.

## 3. DPMSR — threshold-based

1. **Identify** the qualifying transaction: cash **or** international wire at or
   above **AED 55,000**, single or cumulative linked.
2. **Collect** the required data: customer identification, transaction details,
   payment method, counterparty and, where applicable, the linked series.
3. **File** the DPMSR on goAML — regardless of whether the transaction is
   suspicious.
4. **Where the transaction is also suspicious, file an STR as well.**
5. **Record** the confirmation number in the filing log; retain supporting
   documentation for five years.

## 4. TFS reports

PNMR (potential match), CNMR (confirmed match) and FFR (funds freeze) follow the
[`TFS name-match procedure`](../aims/tfs-name-match-procedure.md), which governs
the freeze, the identifier verification and the 5-business-day reporting
deadline. Those filings are recorded in the same log.

## 5. Common filing errors to avoid

1. Incorrect goAML data fields, causing report rejection — verify before submit.
2. Missing cumulative linked transactions across days or connected customers.
3. Confusing DPMSR (threshold) with STR (suspicion) — see the comparison in the
   parent policy.
4. Filing only for cash and overlooking international wire transfers.
5. Missing the reporting deadline while assembling a "complete" picture.
6. A narrative that states a conclusion without the facts that support it.

## 6. Filing log

| Field | Recorded |
|---|---|
| Internal reference | Yes |
| Report type (STR / DPMSR / PNMR / CNMR / FFR / no-action) | Yes |
| Date of escalation and date of filing | Yes |
| Decision-maker | Yes |
| goAML confirmation number | Yes |
| Evidence pack location | Yes |
| Retention expiry (filing + 5 years) | Yes |

The log is reviewed for completeness and timeliness at least annually and is a
standing item in the independent audit scope.
