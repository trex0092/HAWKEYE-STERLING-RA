# UAE Federal AML/CFT/CPF Legal Framework — instrument register

_Reference register of the federal AML laws and executive regulations the
screening engine and Advisor cite. Scope note: these are the federal
instruments applicable to all reporting entities — banks additionally sit
under the CBUAE Rulebook; **DPMS (this product's sector) are supervised by
the Ministry of Economy & Tourism** with the EOCN administering targeted
financial sanctions. Maintained alongside the Regulatory Watch research
notes; verify any instrument against the official portal before legal
reliance. Last verified: 2026-07-14._

| Instrument | What it is | Status / supersession |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal Decree-Law No. (10) of 2025** on Anti-Money Laundering, Combating the Financing of Terrorism and **Proliferation Financing** | The principal AML/CFT/**CPF** law — adds the third (counter-proliferation-financing) pillar, expressly covers virtual assets and digitally-committed offences. Published in the Official Gazette 30/09/2025; **in force 14/10/2025**. | **Current principal law.** Repeals and replaces Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018 as amended (incl. FDL 26/2021 and FDL 7/2024). Verified on the official legislation portal (entry 3314) and the CBUAE Rulebook — see [2026-06 regulatory update](2026-06-aml-regulatory-update.md), items [1]–[5]. |
| **Cabinet Resolution No. (134) of 2025** | The **Executive Regulations** of FDL 10/2025 — the operative CDD, record-keeping, STR and supervisory detail. | **Current.** Supersedes Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019 (the old Implementing Regulation). Official reference via NAMLCFTC. |
| **Cabinet Decision No. (109) of 2023** on beneficial owner procedures | The beneficial-ownership regime: identification, registers, and disclosure duties for legal persons. | Current. Replaced Cabinet Decision No. (58) of 2020. |
| **Cabinet Resolution No. (132) of 2023** | Administrative **penalties for beneficial-owner violations** under 109/2023. | Current. Replaced Cabinet Resolution No. (53) of 2021. |
| **Cabinet Decision No. (74) of 2020** on the UAE List of Terrorists and the implementation of UN Security Council resolutions | The **targeted financial sanctions (TFS)** regime: screening against the UN Consolidated List + UAE Local Terrorist List, freeze **without delay and without prior notice**, no tipping-off, goAML FFR/PNMR reporting, EOCN notification subscription, 50%/control extension. | Current. Administered by the EOCN ([uaeiec.gov.ae](https://www.uaeiec.gov.ae/en/)). This is the list-screening mandate the daily engine executes. |
| **Federal Law No. (7) of 2014** on combating terrorism offences | The substantive counter-terrorism criminal law the terrorist designations rest on. | Current. |

## How the engine maps to this framework

- **Screening & TFS action class** (report §①): Cabinet 74/2020 + FDL 10/2025 —
  UN Consolidated + UAE EOCN Local Terrorist List are core lists; a confirmed
  designation renders the "illegal to engage / freeze without delay / no
  tipping-off / report" instruction.
- **CDD, risk rating, record-keeping, STR prompts**: FDL 10/2025 + Cabinet
  134/2025 (formerly FDL 20/2018-as-amended + Cabinet 10/2019 — the engine's
  narrative citations were migrated to the current instruments on 2026-07-14).
- **UBO extraction & related-party screening**: Cabinet 109/2023 (penalties:
  132/2023).
- **Retention notices**: the firm retains records **10 years** (policy,
  conservative to the statutory minimum) — notices cite the current framework;
  the pre-repeal citation was FDL 26/2021 Art. 23.
- **Proliferation financing**: CPF is a named pillar of FDL 10/2025; the PF
  NRA 2026 circular is tracked in the Regulatory Watch notes.
