# Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) — Hawkeye Sterling (2026)

**Layer 2 — Data Governance Foundation.** **Owner:** MLRO (accountable) · Compliance Engineering.
**Date:** 2026-06-21 · **Frameworks:** UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), GDPR (where applicable).
**Related:** [`data-retention.md`](data-retention.md) · [`ai-asset-register.md`](ai-asset-register.md).

## 1. Purpose & necessity
Hawkeye Sterling performs AML/CFT **entity risk assessment** for a DPMS reporting entity. Processing
of customer and beneficial-owner data is **necessary** to meet statutory AML/CFT obligations
(FDL No.10/2025; Cabinet Resolution 134/2025; FATF R.1, R.10, R.11, R.22). Lawful basis: **legal
obligation** / **public-interest AML mandate**.

## 2. Data categories
KYC/entity data (legal name, jurisdiction, licence, registration); **beneficial-owner PII**
(name, nationality, DOB, passport/Emirates ID, address); screening results (sanctions/PEP/adverse
media); risk scores and analyst overrides; the Advisor's free-text question/context (operator-entered).

## 3. Data flows & recipients
Per [`data-retention.md`](data-retention.md): on-device `localStorage` (primary); **Asana** (task/record
relay); **GitHub** (reference data + fingerprint state, no customer PII); **Anthropic** (Advisor /
reg-draft, transient, no training on inputs). Cross-border transfer: UAE → US (Anthropic) and the
Asana/GitHub cloud regions.

## 4. Risk register

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Residual |
|---|------|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| 1 | PII (passport/EID) pasted into Advisor leaves the device to Anthropic | Med | High | Input caps (4000/2000 chars); operator guidance "don't paste must-not-leave data"; Anthropic no-training; transient processing | Low |
| 2 | `localStorage` exposed on a shared/stolen device | Low | High | AES-256-GCM at rest, passphrase gate, 15-min idle auto-lock | Low |
| 3 | Asana token / Anthropic key leakage | Low | High | Server-held only (never in browser), CORS guard, Gitleaks + CodeQL in CI | Low |
| 4 | Cross-border transfer UAE → US (Anthropic) | Med | Med | Processor terms / no-training API; documented in the asset register; minimise data sent | Med |
| 5 | Over-retention vs the 5-year AML record-keeping rule | Med | Med | Retention schedule + `purgeStaleDraft` auto-purge of abandoned drafts (register records are kept) | Low |
| 6 | Excessive collection by the Advisor (free-text) | Low | Med | Decision-support only; authoritative record is the on-device register, not Advisor chats | Low |

## 5. Data-subject rights
**Access/portability:** in-app JSON export. **Erasure:** register delete + draft purge; clearing the
browser profile erases all local data. **Note:** AML records have a statutory **5-year** retention that
overrides erasure for filed assessments.

## 6. Conclusion & sign-off
Residual risks are **Low–Medium** and proportionate to the AML mandate. Open follow-up: confirm the
Anthropic processor terms reference and re-run this DPIA on any model/provider/backend change.
