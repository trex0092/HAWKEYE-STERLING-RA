## 1. The question

PDPL (FDL 45/2021) permits transfers of personal data outside the UAE to
jurisdictions with adequate protection or, absent adequacy, under appropriate
safeguards (contractual terms, consent, or the further bases in the law and its
Executive Regulations). The firm transfers minimised personal data UAE→US to
two processors: **Anthropic** (AI triage/advisor; API, no-training terms) and
**Asana** (case delivery). The DPIA assesses these transfers as resting on
**processor terms + minimisation** with residual risk Medium, accepted.
**The open question is narrow: is a UAE Data Office transfer approval
additionally required, or do the contractual-safeguard bases suffice?**

## 3. Draft position (for counsel to confirm, amend, or replace)

The transfers proceed under **appropriate contractual safeguards with a
documented transfer-risk assessment**, per the PDPL's non-adequacy transfer
bases; a separate Data Office approval is **not required** so long as (a) the
Executive Regulations, when issued in final form, do not condition the
contractual basis on prior approval for this data class, and (b) the US
recipients' terms remain in force materially unchanged. This position is to be
re-examined on issuance of the Executive Regulations' transfer chapter and on
any material change to either processor's terms.

*Alternative outcome:* if counsel concludes approval **is** required, the ask
routes to the Data Office with the §2 evidence pack, and the DPIA cross-border
row is updated to "approval obtained" with the reference number.
