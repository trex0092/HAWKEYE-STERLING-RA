## 1. Purpose and mandate

The AI Governance Committee is the standing second-line body that governs every
AI system the firm operates or adopts. It owns the approval gate for new AI use
cases, reviews the operated estate against the AI Policy, and advises the Board
on AI risk appetite. It extends the existing AML/CFT compliance programme; it
does not replace any MLRO accountability fixed by law.

## 2. Composition

| Seat | Role |
|---|---|
| Chair | MLRO |
| Member | Compliance Engineering |
| Member | IT / Security |
| Member | Legal |
| Observer (non-voting) | Internal Audit |

Alternates may be named in writing by the Chair. External advisers attend by
invitation, without a vote.

## 3. Cadence

Quarterly as a minimum, aligned to the quarterly model-validation sign-off, and
ad hoc within 5 business days on: a new proposed AI use case, a severity-1 AI
incident, a regulatory change notice from the watchers, or a Board request.
During the remediation plan's Phases 1 to 3 the Committee receives the monthly
progress report described in the Board briefing.

## 4. Responsibilities

1. Approve or reject every proposed AI use case through the register
   onboarding gate (proposed use case, register entry, Committee/MLRO
   approval, controlled deployment, monitored review).
2. Review, quarterly: the AI asset register, eval and bias results, incident
   log, third-party register including DPA status, and the risk register's AI
   rows.
3. Own the enterprise-wide extension of the AI register (plan item 3.3) once
   chartered: inventory any AI use outside this platform, including vendor
   features and shadow AI.
4. Recommend AI risk-appetite changes to the Board; the Board decides.
5. Receive Internal Audit results (the observer seat carries no assurance
   role; independence per [`../aims/internal-audit.md`](../aims/internal-audit.md)
   Section 3).
6. Report to the Board after every sitting: minutes plus a one-page status.

## 5. Quorum and decisions

Quorum is the Chair plus two voting members. Decisions are by majority of
voting members present; the Chair holds the casting vote. Legal-basis
questions (PDPL, sanctions law) are decided only with the Legal seat present.
Minutes are retained 10 years with the compliance records.

## 6. Authority and limits

The Committee may suspend any AI surface immediately via the documented kill
switch and must inform the Board within 24 hours of doing so. It may not
weaken a hard outcome, a retention rule, or human-in-the-loop sign-off;
those changes are Board matters on MLRO recommendation.

## 7. Review

This charter is reviewed annually by the Committee and on any change to the
firm's AI estate or the AI Policy, with amendments adopted the same way the
charter is adopted.
