## Resolutions proposed

Each resolution is drafted for adoption as written, amendment, or rejection.
Strike what the Board does not adopt.

**R1. Ratification record (briefing Phase 0, item 0.2).** The Board confirms
the ratification of AI Policy v1.0, the Stakeholder Impact Assessment and
DPIA Section 6 recorded on 2 July 2026, noting the reconciliation evidence
posted by the workspace owner on the system-of-record task on 16 July 2026;
alternatively the Board ratifies each document afresh by this resolution.

**R2. Phased plan (briefing pages 20 to 24).** The Board approves Phases 1 to
3 of the remediation plan as the governing roadmap, with monthly progress
reporting to the Board once the Committee is chartered.

**R3. Committee charter (plan item 1.2).** The Board adopts the AI Governance
Committee Charter at
[`ai-governance-committee-charter.md`](ai-governance-committee-charter.md)
and directs the Committee to hold its first sitting within 30 days. The Board
notes that adoption unblocks the enterprise-wide AI register extension (plan
item 3.3).

**R4. Phase 2 and 3 resourcing (briefing ask 4).** The Board pre-approves the
resourcing described in the briefing for Phase 2 and Phase 3 so execution does
not wait on a second approval cycle.

**R5. First live evals (plan item 2.2).** The Board notes, and the MLRO
adopts, the live guardrail eval of 13 July 2026 and the live bias eval of
1 July 2026 (both clean, run identifiers recorded in the governance tracker)
as the first live model-assurance evals under the plan, the weekly and
quarterly cadence continuing thereafter.

**R6. ISO/IEC 42001 path (plan item 3.2), decision or deferral.** The Board
either selects a certification path (external readiness assessment first,
full certification, or continued self-assessment with automated evidence) or
defers the decision to a stated date. No recommendation is embedded here; the
Statement of Applicability and the internal-audit programme are the decision
inputs.
